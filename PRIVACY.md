# Privacy Policy for YouTube AI Analyzer Extension

**Last Updated: February 16, 2026**

## Overview

YouTube AI Analyzer is a Chrome extension that helps users analyze YouTube videos using AI technology. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- YouTube video URLs you choose to analyze
- Summary preferences (length percentage)
- Favorite summaries (stored locally in your browser)

### Automatically Collected Information
- Current YouTube page URL (only when you use the extension)
- Extension usage statistics (anonymous)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Generate AI-powered summaries, mind maps, and quizzes
- Improve the extension's functionality
- Provide customer supporthttps://youtube-ai-analyzer.onrender.com

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage**: User preferences and favorites are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API
- **Server Processing**: Video URLs and transcripts are sent to our server (`https://youtube-ai-analyzer.onrender.com`) for AI processing
- **Retention**: Processed data is cached temporarily (7 days) for performance optimization

## Data Sharing

We do NOT:
- Sell your personal information
- Share your data with third parties for marketing
- Track your browsing history outside of YouTube

We DO:
- Use Google Gemini AI API to process video content (Google's privacy policy applies)
- Store anonymous usage statistics

## Third-Party Services

This extension uses:
- **Google Gemini AI**: For AI-powered analysis (https://ai.google.dev/terms)
- **YouTube**: Video content access (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms)

## Your Rights

You can:
- Clear stored data by removing the extension
- Request data deletion by contacting us
- Opt-out by uninstalling the extension

## Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:
- HTTPS encryption for all communications
- Secure server infrastructure
- No storage of sensitive personal information

## Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
